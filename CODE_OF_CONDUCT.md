# Code of conduct

OpenBrick3D is a place to build and play. Everyone who participates, in issues, pull requests, chat, events or the parts registry, agrees to:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; explain, do not scold.
- Welcome newcomers and odd ideas. Exploration is the point.
- Give credit, keep attributions, respect licences.
- Keep it safe: no harassment, discrimination, sexualised content, threats, doxxing or sustained disruption.
- Keep it honest: no misrepresenting measurements, test results or provenance.

Maintainers may edit, remove or decline contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict or ban participants who break these rules, proportionately and with an explanation.

Report concerns privately to conduct@openbrick3d.org (until the domain is live, email work@neuraldeep.net). Reports are handled by two maintainers, kept confidential, and answered within seven days.

This document follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
