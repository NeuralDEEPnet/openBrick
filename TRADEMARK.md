# Trademark policy

The OpenBrick3D name and logo identify this project and the builds its maintainers publish. The MIT licence covers the code; it does not grant rights to the name or logo.

You may:

- Use "OpenBrick3D" to refer to this project, its files and its formats truthfully ("built with OpenBrick3D", "opens OpenBrick3D `.obrick` files").
- Distribute unmodified official builds under the name.
- Name a package in a distribution "openbrick3d" or "openbrick" when it builds this repository's source without functional changes (packagers are credited in README and PACKAGING.md).

You may not:

- Use the name or logo for a modified build, a fork, a service or a product in a way that implies it is official or endorsed; call a fork "OpenBrick3D Pro", "OpenBrick3D Cloud" and so on. Pick your own name and say "based on OpenBrick3D".
- Register domains, social handles, app-store listings or trademarks containing "OpenBrick3D".
- Combine the name or logo with any third-party brick brand's marks.

Neural Deep Network Ltd holds the name on behalf of the project; the merchandise business uses a separate brand. Questions: trademark@openbrick3d.org (until the domain is live, open an issue).
