        Parts

        Licence and credits of the LDraw parts 
        
              
        
              
            
        
            
        Pick a part, then click or tap the ground, a baseplate or another part: it snaps onto the studs. Rotate with R or the button. The ghost is green where it fits and red where it does not; the line at the top of the 3D view says why. The whole ground is the table, a baseplate is just a big plate.
        
            
        
              Keyboard
              
        Tab to the 3D view (it shows a focus outline), then: arrow keys move the aim by one stud, away from you, towards you, left and right as the camera looks; Enter or Space places the part at the aim (the first press only shows the aim); Shift and arrow keys orbit; plus and minus zoom; Home frames the model. Anywhere: R turns the part (Shift+R back), Delete or Backspace removes the part under the pointer or the aim, Ctrl or Cmd+Z undoes, Ctrl or Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes, Escape leaves remove mode.

        Calibration coupon
        
            
        Print this once per printer, nozzle and material. It measures how your printer makes studs, tube bores and 2x2 pockets, so the tiles you print fit genuine bricks. Fit offsets are never applied to the coupon.

        How to use it
        
            
          
              
        1. Slice it flat side down as it comes, without supports, with XY and elephant-foot compensation off (the model has the first-layer chamfer).
          2. 
              
        2. Press a genuine 1x1 plate onto each stud, a turned-over 1x1 plate's stud into each tube bore, and a turned-over 2x2 plate into each pocket. Pick the cell in each line that holds best without force.
          3. 
              
        3. Tap those cells in ; it writes your fit profile here in the browser. Or record them with the command line tool (the values below are examples):
          4. 
            
        
            
        
            
        The command line tool writes the same file; choose “Load my fit profile…” in the logo tile and pick it. The print test guide has the details.
        
          
        
          
        
            
        Cells (first copy)

        LineLabelRecord as
        
                
              
        
            
        
          
        
          
        
          
        
          
        
            
        Record my fit
        
            
        Tap the cell in each line that held best: one stud, one tube bore and one 2x2 pocket (a pocket is one choice: its tube OD and rib depth together). Both copies carry the same cells, so choose from either. With a keyboard: Tab to a line, the arrow keys move and choose.
        
            
        
            
        
            
        
            
        The same value as your choice, in the other copy or the other pocket line.
